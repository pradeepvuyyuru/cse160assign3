Game Mode

Collect all six floating red gas cans, then return to the blue F1 car in the pit lane. The timer starts when you first move.

Quick pickups give a temporary speed boost: within 8 seconds gives 1.3x, within 5 seconds gives 1.6x.

Use W A S D to move, Q/E or mouse drag to turn, and return to the car to launch its victory lap.
